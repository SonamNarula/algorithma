## Why ALGORITHMA

Most DSA trackers count problems. A counter measures activity, not readiness.

ALGORITHMA is built around one harder question:

> **Can you recognize a pattern in a problem you have never seen, and explain why your approach works?**

|  | A typical tracker | ALGORITHMA |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Unit of progress** | Problems solved | Patterns understood |
| **Solved with the editorial open** | Counts the same | Tracked separately as `Soln` |
| **Hard problems** | Forgotten after one attempt | Return through a spaced revision queue |
| **Mistakes** | Not recorded | Logged, then turned into rules |
| **Readiness** | A number that only goes up | A mastery score built from independence and retention |

It sits alongside your handwritten register and rough notebook. It does not try to replace them.

## The 70-day curriculum

Eight phases, from first principles to interview simulation.

<div align="center">
<img src="docs/assets/curriculum.svg" alt="70-day curriculum across eight phases" width="100%" />
</div>

<br />

| Phase | Days | What you cover |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Foundation** | 1-7 | Complexity, arrays, prefix sums, two pointers, sliding window, matrix |
| **Strings and hashing** | 8-14 | HashMap and HashSet, frequency maps, anagrams, string techniques |
| **Linked lists, stacks, queues** | 15-21 | Reversal, fast and slow pointers, cycles, monotonic stack, queue design |
| **Search and recursion** | 22-28 | Binary search, search-space reasoning, first steps in backtracking |
| **Trees** | 29-35 | Traversal, DFS and BFS, BST, heaps and top-K |
| **Graphs and advanced** | 36-56 | Graph traversal, topological sort, union-find, dynamic programming |
| **Specialized patterns** | 57-63 | Bit manipulation, tries, mixed pattern recognition |
| **Interview readiness** | 64-70 | Timed sessions, weak-topic review, mock interviews, final capstone |

The curriculum is derived from a 70-day mind map and refined so every day carries a coherent set of problems. Roadmap, problems, and patterns are plain typed modules, so changing the plan never means touching UI code.

## Philosophy

1. **Patterns over memorization.** Every day is anchored to a pattern, its invariant, and the signals that tell you when to reach for it.
2. **Attempt before you look.** Solo, hint, and solution-viewed are three different outcomes, and they are tracked as such.
3. **Retention over volume.** Revision is a first-class feature, not an afterthought.
4. **Complement the notebook.** Digital notes are optional. Your handwritten register stays the source of truth.
5. **Interview-oriented.** The goal is explaining a solution clearly under time pressure, not growing a counter.

## Your data stays yours

ALGORITHMA is **local-first**. No account, no backend, no tracking.

- Progress lives in your browser and persists across reloads and restarts.
- Data stays on the device and browser you use. It does **not** sync between devices.
- **Settings → JSON export and import** lets you back up progress or move it manually.

> [!WARNING]
> Clearing your browser's site data removes your progress. Export a backup now and then.

## Project structure

```text
algorithma/
├── src/                  # pages, components, data, state
│   └── (typed data)      # roadmap days, problems, patterns
├── scripts/              # project scripts
├── index.html
├── vite.config.ts
├── tailwind.config.js
└── tsconfig.json
```

Curriculum content is kept as typed data under `src/`, separate from the UI. Adjust the plan without touching a component.
